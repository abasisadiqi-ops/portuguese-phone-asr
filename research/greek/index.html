01 / THE TIME AXIS

Every century
changes the question.

No one reconstruction can stand for Mycenaean, Attic, Koine and Byzantine Greek. Date the source and name its dialect.

RULE OF EVIDENCE
Writing survives.
Voices must be argued.

Ancient grammatical descriptions, errors in inscriptions, musical and metrical patterns, and transliteration can constrain our hypotheses. They do not function as recordings.
